Lamplight Heist — a top-down stealth game in a dark museum. Sneak past patrolling guards and their vision cones, use display cases as cover, toss coins to distract them, collect three artifacts and reach the exit unseen.

## How to play

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Click to toss a coin and lure a guard. Stay out of the yellow vision cones — if a guard sees you the detection meter fills, and at full it raises the alarm. Three alarms and the night is over. R restarts, M mutes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
